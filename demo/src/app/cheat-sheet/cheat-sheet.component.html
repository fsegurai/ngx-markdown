#### Link handling With `routerLinkOptions`, clicks on links in the rendered content are handled by ngx-markdown: - the `global` and `paths` `NavigationExtras` are passed to `router.navigate` for `/routerLink:` links and internal paths; - external and `/localFile:` links open in a new tab with `noopener,noreferrer`, so the opened page cannot reach `window.opener`; - Ctrl/Meta/Shift/Alt clicks and middle clicks are left to the browser, so "open in a new tab" works as on a plain page; - `javascript:`, `data:` and `vbscript:` links are never followed (an `[ngx-markdown]` warning is logged), unless `disableRouterLinkHandler` is set.
